1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 11 introduces a comprehensive suite of merchant lifecycle and analytics
cockpit features to GETRA:
1. Workstream 11A (UMKM Headbar & Workspace): Dynamic headbar link and centralized
   overview cockpit (/umkm) displaying verified merchants, active campaigns, and
   submission statuses.
2. Workstream 11B (Merchant Submission Workflow): Full draft staging, MapLibre
   interactive point picker, duplicate candidate warning, and atomic admin approval
   workflow converting staging submissions into canonical verified merchants.
3. Workstream 11C (Campaign Analytics Dashboard): Real-time aggregation of Phase 10
   events into canonical metrics (Impressions, Pin Clicks, Profile Opens, Route
   Requests, Sponsored Pin CTR), daily timeseries trend charts, and placement breakdown.
4. Workstream 11D (Midtrans Sandbox Gate): Formally designated as DEFERRED with full
   architectural justification and criteria roadmap in MIDTRANS_DECISION.txt.
