# GETRA: PHASE 14 DATA DICTIONARY

## 1. Study Areas
- `study_areas`: Polygons defining the bounds of the operational area (e.g. "Kawasan Margonda").

## 2. Transport Network
- `transport_corridors`: LineStrings representing primary transit paths (Angkot routes, BRT paths).
- `transport_nodes`: Points representing stations, terminals, or major hubs.
- `transport_route_stops`: Relational join mapping a corridor to specific node sequences.

## 3. Pedestrian Network
- `pedestrian_nodes`: Topological graph points for walking.
- `pedestrian_edges`: LineStrings connecting pedestrian nodes, with weight/distance properties.
- `transport_access_links`: Virtual edges linking pedestrian nodes to transport nodes for multimodal routing.

## 4. POI & UMKM
- `pois`: Points of Interest (Schools, Malls, Hospitals).
- `umkm_profiles`: Business metadata (Name, Category, Owner) linked to either a POI or a direct spatial point.

## 5. Survey & Demand
- `surveys`: The survey campaigns.
- `survey_responses`: Individual answers (OD matrix endpoints, modal choice preferences).
- `survey_submissions`: Transactional wrappers grouping responses.

## 6. System & Quality
- `dataset_versions`: Logical snapshots defining complete graph states.
- `quality_runs`: Execution logs of validation scripts.
- `quality_check_results`: Pass/Fail lines for individual assertions.
