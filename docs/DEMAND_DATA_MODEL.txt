DEMAND DATA MODEL (Phase 13)

Demand is currently tracked explicitly through synthetic survey responses (Evidence).

1. Raw Survey Evidence
Raw, user-submitted locations (`origin_geometry`, `destination_geometry`) and preferences (`answers` JSONB) directly representing a single trip intent or accessibility profile.

2. Normalized Evidence
During ingestion, raw answers are type-cast, enum-checked, and safely packed into the `answers` JSONB object by the `SurveyService`.

3. Descriptive Aggregate
Instead of creating a new `demand_aggregates` database table prematurely, Phase 13 implements `DemandAggregationService`. This reads the normalized evidence on-the-fly, groupings counts and computing averages. 
By calculating these dynamically, we avoid synchronization issues during testing. 
If query times exceed tolerances in Phase 17/18, this layer will be materialized into a permanent `demand_aggregates` table.

4. Future Demand Intelligence
Phase 13 explicitly stops short of Opportunity Scoring, Ranking, and AI Interpretation. The foundation built here purely provides statistically clean, privacy-aware descriptive data for future AI grounding (Phase 19).
