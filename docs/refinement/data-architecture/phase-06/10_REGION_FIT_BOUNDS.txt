GETRA PHASE 06 - FIT BOUNDS

Single and multi-region bounds come from exact persisted administrative geometry through the Phase 05 region resolver. Multi-region bounds are the deterministic union of selected feature extents; a helper is independently unit-tested.

MapLibre fitBounds uses:
- maxZoom 14 to avoid over-zoom
- desktop padding 52px on all sides
- compact padding top 36, sides 28, bottom 190 to keep geometry above the mobile panel
- 500ms camera transition

Runtime instrumentation confirmed fit calls for single, keyword-region, multi-region, and chip-removal transitions.
