GETRA PHASE 06 - LIMITATIONS AND BLOCKERS

Technical Phase 06 blockers
NONE.

Deployment/legal limitation
The persisted geometry is derived from GADM v4.0. GADM allows academic/non-commercial use but requires prior permission for redistribution or commercial use. Commercial GETRA deployment must obtain permission or migrate to a suitably licensed official boundary source. This limitation does not invalidate the technical Phase 06 behavior but must be resolved before commercial boundary publication.

Data limitations
- five mainland DKI cities only; Kepulauan Seribu is absent from the trusted Phase 05 asset
- city level only; no district/village hierarchy in this contract
- group counts describe only the current globally paginated page
- labels may be collision-hidden on small screens

Rollback
Remove the region summary/grouping wiring and stop passing administrativeBoundaries to GetraMap. Remove the Phase 06 source/layer module and /api/regions route/policy. Existing Phase 05 administrative_regions and canonical merchant data remain untouched. No DB rollback is required.
