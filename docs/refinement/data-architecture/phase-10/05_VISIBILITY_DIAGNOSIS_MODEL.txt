GETRA_VISIBILITY_READINESS_V1

Weights: published 20, category eligibility 20, spatial location 15, opening-hours eligibility 15, price eligibility 15, pedestrian network reachability 15.
The model measures GETRA discovery eligibility, not popularity, revenue, sales, or business quality.
Missing hours means open-now cannot be confirmed. Missing price means hard-budget eligibility cannot be confirmed.
Unroutable or uncovered locations are reported as unavailable network evidence, not visual guesses.
Range and labels use READY >= 80, DEVELOPING >= 50, otherwise INCOMPLETE.
