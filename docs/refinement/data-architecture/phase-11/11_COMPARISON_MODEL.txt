GETRA PHASE 11 - COMPARISON MODEL

Endpoint:
- POST /api/business-space/compare

Request:
- 2 to 4 candidate IDs.
- Canonical category.
- 7 or 30 day window.
- Strict request schema.

Response:
- candidates
- metric_rows
- trade_off_summary
- model_version
- limitations

Rules:
- Candidates are compared with the same category and demand window.
- Comparison does not rank by expected profit.
- Comparison exposes trade-offs only.
