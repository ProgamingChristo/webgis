The deterministic parser returns domain, keyword, location_text, scope, category,
constraints, validated origin, parser, and confidence. Supported Indonesian budget
forms include 30 ribu, 30rb, Rp30.000, and 20 ribuan. Walking limits are normalized
to minutes. Opening intent is represented as an explicit open-now constraint.
