GETRA PHASE 08 - SMART SEARCH AI FALLBACK

Structured search does not depend on an AI provider. Deterministic parsing is the
canonical fallback and remains usable when AI is absent or fails. AI is restricted
to grounded explanation of already verified GIS output. During browser verification
the provider was unavailable, so GETRA returned a safe deterministic explanation:
the values came from the pgRouting result, not from model estimation.
