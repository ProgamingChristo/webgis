GROUNDED AI INTERPRETATION

The interpretation endpoint recomputes analytics on the server and selects one aggregate row.
It never accepts client-provided scores as facts. Any AI response is rejected unless its numbers
belong to server evidence and it contains no revenue, profit, ROI, total-demand, or guarantee claim.

If AI is unavailable or rejected, a deterministic explanation is returned. Browser evidence observed
DETERMINISTIC_FALLBACK with the exact selected category, region, confidence, sample, and null gap.
Analytics maps, charts, and tables remain usable independently of AI.
