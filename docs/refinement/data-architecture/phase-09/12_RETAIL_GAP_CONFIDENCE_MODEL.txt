RETAIL GAP CONFIDENCE MODEL

sample < 3: INSUFFICIENT_DATA, Retail Gap null.
sample < 10 OR source diversity < 2: LIMITED_EVIDENCE.
sample >= 30 AND source diversity >= 3: STRONGER_EVIDENCE.
otherwise: MODERATE_EVIDENCE.

Every row returns sample size, source diversity/types, latest signal time, and coverage status.
Confidence qualifies evidence breadth; it does not claim statistical population confidence.
