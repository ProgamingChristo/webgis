SUPPLY SIGNAL MODEL

Supply is COUNT(DISTINCT canonical merchant id) for published canonical merchants matching the
same category and administrative polygon. This preserves Phase 03 reconciliation deduplication.
Premium and Menu Go are not counted as separate merchants after canonical reconciliation.

supply_score uses the same logarithmic five-region normalization as demand_score and is bounded 0..100.
It represents supply currently represented in GETRA, not a complete business census.
