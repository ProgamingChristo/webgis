RETAIL GAP FORMULA

Model version: GETRA_RETAIL_GAP_V1.
retail_gap = demand_score - supply_score, bounded -100..100.
Positive means observed demand evidence is relatively higher than represented supply.
Negative means represented supply is relatively higher than observed demand evidence.

Retail Gap is null when raw demand sample size is below 3. It is not a revenue, profit,
ROI, guaranteed opportunity, investment recommendation, or property forecast.
