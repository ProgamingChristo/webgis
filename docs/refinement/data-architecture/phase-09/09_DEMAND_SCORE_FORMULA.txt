DEMAND SCORE FORMULA

Model version: GETRA_DEMAND_V1.
weighted_demand = search*1 + route*2 + commuter*1.5 + transaction_observation*2.
demand_score = round(100 * ln(1 + region_weighted_demand) /
                     ln(1 + maximum_weighted_demand_across_5_regions)).
When the denominator is zero, score is zero. Output is bounded 0..100.

The score is relative observed GETRA/platform demand evidence. It is not total population demand,
market size, sales, revenue, profit, ROI, or a forecast.
