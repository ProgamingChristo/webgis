ECHARTS ANALYTICS

ECharts 6 renders three horizontally navigable views:
1. Demand Score versus Supply Score (0..100).
2. Retail Gap (-100..100).
3. Raw signal composition for the selected region.

Each chart has an accessible label, stable 210px height, ResizeObserver lifecycle, and textual table.
Raw counts and normalized scores are never placed on a shared misleading axis.
Browser evidence confirmed three rendered canvases with non-zero pixel areas.
