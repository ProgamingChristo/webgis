MISSION PROVENANCE MODEL

Each observation preserves:
- provider: MAPID
- source_type
- source_record_id
- imported_at

Additional trace:
- raw_payload JSONB
- raw_payload_checksum SHA-256
- mission_name when available
- observed_at when available
- provider_updated_at when available
- latest_sync_run_id

Claim boundary:
- SOURCE_OBSERVED does not mean verified truth.
- Menu Go remains merchant observation.
- Struk Go remains transaction observation.
- Properti Go remains property observation.
- Activities remains field observation.
