UMKM ADVERTISING — KNOWN LIMITATIONS & EXPLICIT DEFERRALS

1. PAYMENT GATEWAY BOUNDARIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Midtrans Sandbox Only: Live customer charging is intentionally disabled in Phase 14.
- Fixed Nominal: Commercial dynamic pricing tiers are deferred; technical test nominal
  (Rp 50.000) is used for Sandbox verification.
- Local Webhooks: External Midtrans webhooks in local development require a public HTTPS
  tunnel (e.g. ngrok) or manual server-side refresh via the "Cek Status" action button.

2. ANALYTICS BOUNDARIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Interaction Telemetry Only: GETRA Analytics measures user attention and map interactions
  (Impressions, Pin Clicks, Profile Opens, Route Requests).
- Not Sales / ROI: Does not track physical merchant POS transactions, revenue, profit,
  or financial Return on Investment (ROI/ROAS).

3. SPATIAL & ROUTING BOUNDARIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- PostGIS & pgRouting Authority: Spatial distance and route geometry calculations are
  computed strictly by PostGIS algorithms; AI is utilized solely for contextual insights.
