GETRA — GEO-ENABLED TRANSIT & RETAIL ANALYTICS
UMKM ADVERTISING — TROUBLESHOOTING & DIAGNOSTIC GUIDE
================================================================================

1. AUTHENTICATION & HEADBAR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Case 1: "UMKM" link missing from Headbar
- Symptom: Headbar shows only General, Community, Profile.
- Likely Cause: User profile does not have 'UMKM' mode in `user_stakeholder_modes`.
- Safe Fix: Navigate to Profile/Onboarding settings or update `user_stakeholder_modes` for user.

Case 2: 401 Unauthorized / Session expired
- Symptom: API requests fail with 401.
- Safe Fix: Sign out and sign in again at `/login` to refresh Supabase JWT session.

2. MERCHANT SUBMISSION & MAPLIBRE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Case 3: MapLibre location picker not rendering
- Symptom: Blank gray box instead of map canvas.
- Likely Cause: Missing `NEXT_PUBLIC_MAPID_BASEMAP_KEY` or MapLibre GL worker script.
- Safe Fix: Verify `.env.local` contains valid map key and ensure `copy-maplibre-worker.mjs` ran during build.

Case 4: Submission stuck in "Menunggu Review"
- Symptom: Submission submitted but merchant does not appear in "Merchant Saya".
- Likely Cause: Submission awaiting admin approval.
- Safe Fix: Use Admin account to call `POST /api/admin/merchant-submissions/:id/approve`.

3. MIDTRANS SANDBOX PAYMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Case 5: Snap popup does not open
- Symptom: Clicking "Bayar dengan Midtrans" shows error or does nothing.
- Likely Cause: `MIDTRANS_SERVER_KEY` or `NEXT_PUBLIC_MIDTRANS_CLIENT_KEY` missing.
- Safe Fix: Check `.env.local` and ensure valid Sandbox keys are present.

Case 6: Payment state remains "PENDING" after Snap completion
- Symptom: Snap popup closed but panel still shows "Menunggu Pembayaran".
- Safe Fix: Click "Cek Status" on the payment panel to trigger real-time status reconciliation from Midtrans Status API.

4. AD SERVING & FAIR DISCOVERY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Case 7: Sponsored Pin not visible on General Map
- Symptom: Campaign is active but pin does not render on commuter map.
- Likely Cause: Map center is outside campaign targeting radius, or campaign schedule has not started.
- Safe Fix: Pan map closer to merchant coordinates, or update campaign start_at to current time.
