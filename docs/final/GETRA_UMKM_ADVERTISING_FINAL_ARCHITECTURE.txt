2. ARCHITECTURAL PILLARS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. GIS Is Authority, AI Is Interpreter:
   - Spatial containment, proximity radii, and pedestrian route geometries are computed
     deterministically by PostGIS and pgRouting.
2. Fair Discovery & Organic Preservation:
   - Sponsored placements are strictly limited (max 1 per discovery response) and clearly labeled.
   - Payment never alters organic ranking scores or creates artificial "Hidden Gem" badges.
3. Commuter Privacy & Zero PII:
   - Append-only event tracking records interaction counts only, with zero commuter user IDs
     or GPS movement histories.
