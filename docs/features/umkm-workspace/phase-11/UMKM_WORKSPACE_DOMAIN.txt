GETRA — GEO-ENABLED TRANSIT & RETAIL ANALYTICS
UMKM WORKSPACE — DOMAIN MODEL SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

1. DOMAIN PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The UMKM Workspace provides a unified management surface for micro, small, and
medium enterprises (UMKM) operating physical retail and food & beverage outlets
around transit corridors.

2. CORE ENTITIES & RELATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. User Context:
   - Identifies whether an authenticated account has activated 'UMKM' mode.
   - Distinct from merchant ownership.

2. Owned Verified Merchants:
   - Canonical merchant entities in public.merchants where `owner_id = user.id`.
   - Represents legally/administratively verified businesses capable of running
     promotional campaigns.

3. Merchant Submissions (Draft & Pending):
   - In-flight business registration records in public.merchant_submissions.
   - Tracks curation status (DRAFT, PENDING_REVIEW, APPROVED, REJECTED, CANCELLED).

4. Active Promotional Campaigns:
   - Live ad campaigns in public.ad_campaigns linked to owned merchants.
   - Drives commuter discovery and map sponsored pin placements.

3. WORKSPACE AGGREGATE STRUCTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
UmkmWorkspaceSummary:
├── verified_merchants_count: number
├── active_campaigns_count: number
├── pending_submissions_count: number
├── owned_merchants: OwnedMerchantItem[]
└── recent_submissions: SubmissionSummaryItem[]
