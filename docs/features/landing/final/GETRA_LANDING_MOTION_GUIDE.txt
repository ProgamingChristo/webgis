GETRA LANDING MOTION GUIDE

Motion principles:
- Subtle, spatial, and purposeful.
- Route draw and marker pulse should support WebGIS comprehension.
- Avoid heavy animation that distracts from content.

Files:
- frontend/app/globals.css contains scoped landing animation primitives.
- Components apply classes such as getra-reveal and getra-route-draw.

Accessibility:
- prefers-reduced-motion is supported.
- Do not add critical information that only appears through animation.
- Keep keyboard focus states visible.
