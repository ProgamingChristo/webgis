Problem model
-------------
The section uses real problem classes rather than an invented company founding story.

1. Distance != Access
   A point that looks nearby by straight-line distance may not be simple to reach on a pedestrian network.

2. Demand & Supply Terpisah
   Commuter demand and nearby merchant supply often live in disconnected systems.

3. Popularitas != Relevansi
   Popular or promoted places are not always the most relevant under the user's constraints.

4. Data Membutuhkan Freshness
   Prices, opening hours, pedestrian conditions, merchant status, and area context can change over time.

Visual model
------------
The visual is intentionally spatial: transit point, walking path, merchant point, and GIS layer cards. It avoids generic white cards and does not imply live production telemetry.
