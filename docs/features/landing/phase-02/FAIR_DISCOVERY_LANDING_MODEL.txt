GETRA LANDING PHASE 02
FAIR DISCOVERY LANDING MODEL

Section model
-------------
Fair Discovery explains how GETRA separates:
- Original
- Hidden Gem
- Sponsored

Hard constraints
----------------
Sponsored results must still pass contextual constraints before being shown:
- location
- category
- price
- open now
- walking limit

Sponsored disclosure
--------------------
Sponsored content remains visibly labeled. Payment is not described as silently replacing organic relevance.

Event safety
------------
The landing preview does not create advertising events:
- no IMPRESSION
- no SPONSORED_PIN_CLICK
- no PROFILE_OPEN
- no ROUTE_REQUEST
