PHASE 08 — FAIR DISCOVERY ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

1. EXECUTIVE ARCHITECTURE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 08 establishes the Fair Discovery subdomain in GETRA, integrating organic
transit discoverability, community-curated Hidden Gems, and paid Sponsored Placements
into a unified, fair, and transparent search experience for commuters.

Key architectural boundaries:
- Subdomain Separation:
  * Fair Discovery: `backend/src/features/fair-discovery/` and `frontend/src/features/fair-discovery/`
  * Advertising Engine: `backend/src/features/umkm-advertising/`
- Zero Organic Score Contamination:
  * Advertising revenue or campaign status NEVER modifies organic relevance scoring.
  * `score += advertisingWeight` is strictly forbidden.
- Clear Stream Partitioning:
  * Stream 1: Original (Organic) — Pure spatial proximity and hard constraints.
  * Stream 2: Hidden Gem — Curated by community data quality & transit engagement.
  * Stream 3: Sponsored — Commercial campaign placement satisfying exact search context.

2. COMPOSITION PIPELINE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Commuter initiates search or pans map:
   Input: { origin: [lng, lat], radiusMeters, category, query, openNow, maxWalkingMinutes }
2. `FairDiscoveryCompositionService` queries organic merchants within radius.
3. Partitions organic candidates into `original` and `hidden_gems` (data_quality_score >= 80).
4. Calls `SponsoredPlacementAdapter` (consuming public `AdServingService.getSponsoredPinCandidates`).
5. Applies Hard-Constraint Parity on sponsored candidates (Category, Query, Walking Limit).
6. Deduplicates: Removes sponsored merchant from duplicate organic list appearance.
7. Clamps sponsored candidates to `MAX_SPONSORED_RESULTS_PER_DISCOVERY = 1`.
8. Emits `FairDiscoveryResult` to frontend API response.
