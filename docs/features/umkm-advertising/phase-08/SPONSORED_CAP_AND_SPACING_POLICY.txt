UMKM INTELLIGENCE & ADVERTISING TRACK
PHASE 08 — SPONSORED CAP & SPACING POLICY
================================================================================

1. DENSITY RESTRICTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To protect commuter trust and ensure organic discoverability remains dominant:
- `MAX_SPONSORED_RESULTS_PER_DISCOVERY = 1` (strict MVP limit).
- At most 1 sponsored card is rendered per search query response.
- On the MapLibre map viewport, sponsored pins are capped to avoid crowding out
  transit nodes and organic POIs.

2. FAIR DISCOVERY METRICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The API response returns `metadata`:
- `total_original`: count of regular organic merchants
- `total_hidden_gems`: count of curated hidden gems
- `total_sponsored`: count of active sponsored placements (0 or 1)
- `sponsored_available`: boolean indicator for quick frontend rendering checks
