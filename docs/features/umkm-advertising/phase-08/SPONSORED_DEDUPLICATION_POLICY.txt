UMKM INTELLIGENCE & ADVERTISING TRACK
PHASE 08 — SPONSORED DEDUPLICATION POLICY
================================================================================

1. DEDUPLICATION GOAL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To prevent commuter confusion and interface clutter, a physical merchant
MUST NOT appear twice in the search results (e.g. appearing in both Sponsored
and Original lists simultaneously).

2. POLICY SPECIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Maximum 1 Sponsored placement per physical merchant ID.
2. If a merchant ID is selected in the `sponsored` bucket:
   - The merchant is retained in the `sponsored` list.
   - The duplicate instance is filtered out from `original` and `hidden_gems`.
3. Relative Order Preservation:
   - Removing the sponsored merchant from organic results DOES NOT alter
     the relative sort order or distance metrics of any other remaining organic merchant.
   - Remaining merchants maintain their exact organic ranking order.
