# FINAL DOCUMENTATION PLAN (UMKM ADVERTISING)

Phase 12 will assemble the official guides. Ensure the following is covered:

1. **How to verify Ownership**: Covered by Phase 2 documentation.
2. **How to create a Campaign Draft**: Covered by Phase 3. The UMKM user clicks "Buat Campaign Draft" in the Advertising Manager.
3. **Campaign Route**: `/umkm/advertising` with a valid `merchantId`.
4. **Campaign API**: Documented in `CAMPAIGN_API.txt`.
5. **Campaign Lifecycle**: Currently only DRAFT and CANCELLED. Future phases will expand this.
6. **How to create a Creative**: Documented in Phase 4. Use the "Kelola Creative" button on a DRAFT campaign.
7. **Creative Types**: MVP supports `SPONSORED_PIN`.
8. **Image Requirements**: Optional for SPONSORED_PIN. Max 5MB, JPG/PNG/WEBP.
9. **Creative READY vs Campaign ACTIVE**: Creative `READY` means the content passes validation. Campaign `ACTIVE` means the ad is currently being served on the map (Not implemented yet).
10. **How to select Campaign Area**: Documented in Phase 5. Use the "🎯 Kelola Targeting" button on a DRAFT campaign.
11. **Radius Targeting Explanation**: Proximity around merchant outlet (250m - 10 km). Center location is authoritative from merchant data.
12. **Study Area Targeting Explanation**: Target full predefined GETRA transit/operational zones.
13. **Map Preview**: MapLibre rendering with dynamic circle buffer and study area polygons.
14. **Common Targeting Errors**: Missing merchant location coordinates, radius out of range, attempting to edit non-DRAFT campaign.
15. **Fair Discovery Integration**: Documented in Phase 8 (`docs/features/umkm-advertising/phase-08/`). Separates Original, Hidden Gem, and Sponsored streams. Organic immutability guaranteed; hard constraints strictly enforced. Max 1 sponsored result per discovery response. Deduplication removes duplicate visual occurrence.
16. **Contextual Promo Banner & Profile Poster**: Documented in Phase 9 (`docs/features/umkm-advertising/phase-09/`). Adds Contextual Promo Banner to Fair Discovery feed (capped to 1 per context) and Profile Poster to merchant detail drawer (strictly same-merchant identity). Reuses canonical Campaign, Creative, Targeting, and Lifecycle foundations. Zero event tracking side effects.
17. **Campaign Interaction Event Tracking**: Documented in Phase 10 (`docs/features/umkm-advertising/phase-10/`). Establishes append-only `campaign_events` table for 4 canonical interaction events (`IMPRESSION`, `SPONSORED_PIN_CLICK`, `PROFILE_OPEN`, `ROUTE_REQUEST`). Explains viewport-based visibility rules, map visibility rules, database-level deduplication, privacy protection (no `user_id` or GPS history), and why interaction analytics are distinct from revenue/ROI.
18. **UMKM Workspace, Merchant Submission, and Campaign Analytics**: Documented in Phase 11 (`docs/features/umkm-workspace/phase-11/`, `docs/features/merchant-submission/phase-11/`, `docs/features/umkm-advertising/phase-11/`). Provides dynamic Headbar navigation for UMKM mode users, centralized UMKM Workspace (`/umkm`), complete merchant registration workflow with MapLibre interactive location picker and atomic admin approval, and real-time Campaign Analytics dashboard (`/umkm/advertising/analytics`) visualizing commuter responses, CTR, timeseries trends, and placement breakdowns with strict zero-PII privacy enforcement. Midtrans sandbox gate is formally deferred with architectural justification.
19. **Midtrans Sandbox Payment Integration**: Documented in Phase 12 (`docs/features/umkm-advertising/phase-12/`). Establishes server-authoritative Snap transaction creation, popup payment UI with Sandbox badges, robust PostgreSQL `ad_payment_orders` persistence with RLS, SHA-512 digital signature verification on HTTP notifications, strict amount/order verification, idempotency and out-of-order protection, and integrated payment readiness for advertising campaigns without compromising organic discovery or commuter privacy.
