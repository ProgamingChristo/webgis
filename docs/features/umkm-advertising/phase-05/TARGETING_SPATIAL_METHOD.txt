# TARGETING SPATIAL METHOD & GIS PRINCIPLES (PHASE 5)

## Spatial Authority
"GIS calculates. Frontend visualizes."
The backend database and PostGIS are authoritative for all coordinate, bounding box, buffer, and intersection calculations. The frontend only displays polygons and markers received from the backend API.

## Coordinate Standard
- Coordinate System: EPSG:4326 (WGS 84).
- GeoJSON Coordinate Order: `[longitude, latitude]`.

## Units of Measurement
- Distance Unit: METERS (m).
- Calculations on Earth surface use `geography` type in PostGIS (meter-accurate spherical/spheroidal geodesics) rather than degree-based geometry operations.

## PostGIS Functions & RPC
1. **Radius Buffer Computation**:
   ```sql
   ST_Buffer(
     ST_SetSRID(ST_MakePoint(lng, lat), 4326)::geography,
     radius_m
   )::geometry
   ```
2. **GeoJSON Export**:
   `ST_AsGeoJSON(...)` converted to RFC 7946 GeoJSON.
3. **RPC Function**:
   `public.generate_radius_buffer_geojson(lng, lat, radius_m)` returns a GeoJSON Polygon for preview.

## Radius Bounds & Limits
- `MIN_RADIUS_METERS`: 250 meters. (Rationale: Pedestrian neighborhood radius, ~3-4 minute walk).
- `MAX_RADIUS_METERS`: 10,000 meters / 10 km. (Rationale: Urban catchment area, transit-connected commuter scale).
- `DEFAULT_RADIUS_METERS`: 1,000 meters / 1 km.

## Study Area Semantics
- Uses `public.study_areas.geometry` (MultiPolygon, 4326).
- Validated via `is_valid_wgs84_geometry(geometry, ARRAY['MULTIPOLYGON']::text[])`.
- Later ad serving spatial matching will use `ST_Covers(study_area.geometry, user_location)`.
