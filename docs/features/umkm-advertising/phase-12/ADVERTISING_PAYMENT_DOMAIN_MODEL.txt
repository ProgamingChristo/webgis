1. DOMAIN SEPARATION PRINCIPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Payment Order ≠ Advertising Campaign:
  * A Campaign (`public.ad_campaigns`) defines promotion content, targeting, and schedule.
  * A Payment Order (`public.ad_payment_orders`) records a financial transaction attempt.
  * Payment status is NOT added to the campaign lifecycle status enum.

- Payment Status ≠ Campaign Lifecycle Status:
  * Payment Statuses: `CREATED`, `PENDING`, `PAID`, `FAILED`, `EXPIRED`, `CANCELLED`, `REFUNDED`.
  * Campaign Statuses: `DRAFT`, `READY`, `SCHEDULED`, `ACTIVE`, `PAUSED`, `ENDED`, `CANCELLED`.
  * A campaign reaching `PAID` payment state does NOT automatically become `ACTIVE`; it must
    still satisfy creative readiness, targeting validity, and schedule boundaries.

- Payment ≠ Organic Search / Ranking Boost:
  * Paid campaigns remain explicitly labeled as `Sponsored`.
  * Payment does NOT grant "Hidden Gem" or "Original" organic ranking badges.
  * Fair Discovery hard constraints and geographic relevance filters remain non-negotiable.
