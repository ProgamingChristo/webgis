UMKM ADVERTISING — CAMPAIGN PAYMENT READINESS SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

1. PAYMENT READINESS EVALUATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Executed by `CampaignPaymentEligibilityService`:
- `is_ready = true` ONLY IF `ad_payment_orders` contains a record with `status = 'PAID'`.
- If status is `PENDING`, `CREATED`, `FAILED`, `EXPIRED`, `CANCELLED`, or no order exists:
  `is_ready = false`.

2. COMPLETE CAMPAIGN READINESS MATRIX
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To transition a campaign to `READY` / `SCHEDULED` / `ACTIVE`:
1. Merchant Eligibility: Verified merchant ownership & active merchant profile.
2. Creative Readiness: Valid headline, body, and promotional image asset.
3. Targeting Readiness: Defined transit study area and pedestrian proximity buffer.
4. Schedule Validity: `start_at < end_at` within acceptable campaign windows.
5. Payment Readiness: Verified `PAID` payment order in Midtrans Sandbox.
