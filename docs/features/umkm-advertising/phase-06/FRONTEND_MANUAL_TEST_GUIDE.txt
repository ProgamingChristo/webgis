================================================================================
GETRA — GEO-ENABLED TRANSIT & RETAIL ANALYTICS
UMKM INTELLIGENCE & ADVERTISING TRACK — PHASE 06
FRONTEND MANUAL TESTING & VERIFICATION GUIDE
================================================================================

1. PREREQUISITES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Start backend server:
   `cd backend && npm run dev` (running on http://localhost:3001)
2. Start frontend server:
   `cd frontend && npm run dev` (running on http://localhost:3000)
3. Log in as an authenticated user, or navigate directly to:
   `http://localhost:3000/umkm/advertising`


2. STEP-BY-STEP MANUAL TEST WALKTHROUGH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Step 1: Select or Claim a Demo Merchant
---------------------------------------
1. In the UMKM Advertising Dashboard (`/umkm/advertising`), locate the
   "Pilih Toko yang Sudah Dimiliki" or "Rekomendasi Toko Siap Iklan" card.
2. Select or click "Klaim & Kelola Toko Ini" for "Kopi Toko Djawa - Braga".
3. Verify that the merchant card displays "Eligible untuk Advertising" with
   green checkmarks.

Step 2: Create a New Campaign
-----------------------------
1. Under "Buat Campaign Baru", enter:
   - Nama Campaign: "Promo Kopi Pagi Braga"
   - Deskripsi: "Diskon 20% bagi commuter transit stasiun Braga"
2. Click "Buat Campaign".
3. Verify the new campaign appears in the list with a "Draft" slate badge.

Step 3: Test Readiness Checklist
--------------------------------
1. Click "Jadwal & Kesiapan" on the new campaign card.
2. Observe `CampaignReadinessPanel`:
   - Kelayakan Toko: [✓] (Passed)
   - Materi Iklan: [!] (Incomplete)
   - Target Wilayah: [!] (Incomplete)
   - Jadwal Waktu: [!] (Incomplete)
   - Progress bar: 25%.

Step 4: Configure Creative
--------------------------
1. Click "Kelola Materi".
2. Add a new creative headline: "Ngopi Pagi Diskon 20%".
3. Click "Tandai Siap Tayang (Ready)".
4. Return to "Jadwal & Kesiapan" tab -> verify Creative check is now [✓] (50%).

Step 5: Configure Spatial Targeting
-----------------------------------
1. Click "Targeting Wilayah".
2. Adjust radius slider to "750 meter".
3. Click "Simpan Pengaturan Targeting".
4. Return to "Jadwal & Kesiapan" tab -> verify Targeting check is now [✓] (75%).

Step 6: Set Campaign Schedule & Presets
---------------------------------------
1. In the "Jadwal & Kesiapan" tab, look at `CampaignScheduleEditor`.
2. Click the preset button "+7 Hari (1 Minggu)".
3. Verify the start and end dates are populated and the duration calculation
   shows "7 hari (168 jam)".
4. Click "Simpan Jadwal".
5. Verify:
   - Green success alert appears: "Jadwal campaign berhasil disimpan!".
   - Readiness progress reaches 100% [✓ Siap Tayang].
   - Status badge in the campaign header dynamically updates to "Terjadwal"
     (or "Aktif Berjalan" if start date is current).

Step 7: Test Pause, Resume, and Cancel Actions
----------------------------------------------
1. In the campaign header, click "Jeda Campaign (Pause)".
2. Verify status badge updates to amber "Dijeda (Paused)".
3. Click "Lanjutkan Tayang (Resume)".
4. Verify status badge returns to "Terjadwal" or "Aktif Berjalan".
5. Click "Batalkan Campaign".
6. Verify confirmation modal appears.
7. Click "Ya, Batalkan Campaign".
8. Verify status updates to red "Dibatalkan" and action buttons disappear.
