# CREATIVE DOMAIN MODEL (PHASE 4)

## Campaign vs Creative
- **Campaign** is the business container (who owns it, when it runs, its lifecycle).
- **Creative** is the actual promotional content (what it says, the image, the CTA).
- Relationship: 1 Campaign can have multiple Creatives, but in Phase 4 MVP, we enforce a strict 1-to-1 mapping per `creative_type` to prevent version explosion.

## Creative vs Placement
- A Creative defines "what the ad says".
- It does NOT define "where the ad appears" (e.g. Map, Feed, Search). Placements are handled in future phases (Phase 5+, 7, 9).

## Creative Types
- `SPONSORED_PIN`: The primary MVP target for MapLibre rendering. Requires a headline and CTA. Image is optional.
- `CONTEXTUAL_BANNER`: Reserved for future use.
- `PROFILE_POSTER`: Reserved for future use.

## Creative Status
- `DRAFT`: The initial state. Content can be edited freely.
- `READY`: The merchant has finished drafting the content and it passes all validation. (Note: READY != ACTIVE. The campaign itself may still be DRAFT).

## Call to Action (CTA)
- Restricted to safe, internal semantic values to prevent external phishing/redirects.
- Allowed values: `VIEW_PROFILE`, `REQUEST_ROUTE`.
- The frontend will map these to localized labels (e.g., "Lihat Profil", "Minta Rute").

## Media (Image)
- Images are stored securely in Supabase Storage under a managed path convention.
- Direct public URLs are generated post-upload.
