1. SPATIAL MATCHING STRATEGIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. RADIUS TARGETING (Geodesic Distance)
   - Evaluates whether context coordinate (user/map center) is within `radius_meters`
     of campaign target center (or merchant location fallback).
   - Math: Haversine distance formula on WGS84 sphere (R = 6,371,000 meters).
   - Authoritative Condition: `distance_meters(context, center) <= radius_meters`.

B. STUDY AREA TARGETING (Polygon Containment)
   - Evaluates whether context coordinate is topologically inside the predefined
     Study Area polygon/multipolygon.
   - Algorithm: Robust Ray-Casting Point-in-Polygon (PIP) with hole exclusion.
   - Authoritative Condition: `isPointInsideStudyArea(study_area_id, lng, lat) === true`.

2. COORDINATE CONVENTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Standard: WGS84 (EPSG:4326)
- Order: GeoJSON standard `[longitude, latitude]`
- Bounds Check:
  - Longitude: [-180.0, 180.0]
  - Latitude: [-90.0, 90.0]
  - Non-finite (NaN, Infinity) rejected with AdServingContextInvalidError (HTTP 400).
