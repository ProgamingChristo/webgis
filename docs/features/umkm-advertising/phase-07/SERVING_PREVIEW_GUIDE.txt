1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Serving Preview Panel allows UMKM merchants to test and simulate whether their
campaign will be shown to users at different geographic coordinates.

Features:
1. "Gunakan Titik Toko": Quick shortcut to test coordinates directly at the merchant's physical address.
2. Custom Coordinates / Map Dragging: Allows clicking or dragging the "Titik Uji" pin on the map to test edge of radius boundaries.
3. 4-Pillar Diagnostic Checklist:
   - 1. Status (ACTIVE vs Draft/Scheduled/Paused/Ended)
   - 2. Toko UMKM (Eligible vs Incomplete claim)
   - 3. Materi Iklan (Sponsored Pin Ready vs Incomplete)
   - 4. Targeting (Inside vs Outside target area)
4. Live Pin & Card Preview:
   Renders the exact Sponsored Pin on the map and Sponsored Pin Card when servable.
5. Zero Event Side Effects:
   Owner previews do NOT trigger impressions, clicks, or billable ad events.
