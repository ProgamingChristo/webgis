AD SERVING ARCHITECTURE

1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 07 introduces GETRA's Ad Serving Engine and Sponsored Pin domain.
The core responsibility of Phase 07 is answering one authoritative question:
"Which advertising campaign is technically and spatially eligible to be served
as a Sponsored Pin at a specific coordinate [longitude, latitude]?"

Phase 07 purposefully does NOT handle ad ranking composition against Organic/Hidden Gems
(which belongs to Phase 08 Fair Discovery), nor does it track impression/click events
(which belongs to Phase 10 Analytics).


2. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & DATA FLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[ Client / Map Context ]
         │ (longitude, latitude, limit)
         ▼
[ GET /api/advertising/placements/sponsored-pins ]
         │
         ▼
[ AdServingService ]
         │
         ├── 1. Context Coordinate Validation (WGS84 EPSG:4326)
         │
         ├── 2. Candidate Retrieval (AdServingRepository.findPotentialServingCampaigns)
         │       └── Filter: status NOT IN ('CANCELLED', 'ENDED')
         │
         ├── 3. Candidate Serving Verification Pipeline (Per Campaign)
         │       ├── A. Lifecycle Status: CampaignLifecycleService.getEffectiveCampaignStatus === 'ACTIVE'
         │       ├── B. Merchant Eligibility: AdvertisingEligibilityService.verifyEligibility === true
         │       ├── C. Creative Asset: Type SPONSORED_PIN, Status READY, Non-empty Headline, CTA Valid
         │       └── D. Spatial Targeting: Point-in-Radius (Haversine) OR Point-in-Study-Area (Ray-casting)
         │
         ├── 4. Merchant Deduplication
         │       └── Max 1 Sponsored Pin per Merchant (Tie-break: most recently updated campaign)
         │
         ├── 5. Technical Ordering & Limiting
         │       └── Deterministic updated_at DESC, clamp limit [1..20] (default: 5)
         │
         ▼
[ Safe Public SponsoredPinDTO[] ] (Server-enforced label: "Sponsored", zero sensitive leaks)
