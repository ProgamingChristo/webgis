GETRA - UMKM INTELLIGENCE & ADVERTISING TRACK
PHASE 09: REUSE OF CAMPAIGN, CREATIVE, LIFECYCLE & TARGETING
============================================================

1. CANONICAL CAMPAIGN REUSE
- Single campaign entity in `ad_campaigns` manages budget, status, start/end dates for all surfaces.
- A single merchant campaign can host:
  - `SPONSORED_PIN` creative (for map pin ad serving)
  - `CONTEXTUAL_BANNER` creative (for search/discovery feeds)
  - `PROFILE_POSTER` creative (for merchant profile view)

2. CREATIVE REUSE & CONSTRAINTS
- Reuses `ad_creatives` table.
- Enforces `UNIQUE(campaign_id, creative_type)`.
- Status transitions (`DRAFT` -> `READY`) apply uniformly across creative types.

3. LIFECYCLE & SCHEDULE REUSE
- Pausing a campaign (`POST /api/umkm/advertising/campaigns/[id]/pause`) automatically halts all 3 placement types simultaneously.
- Resuming a campaign resumes all ready placements.
- Reaching `end_at` automatically expires all placements in lockstep.

4. SPATIAL TARGETING REUSE
- `ad_campaign_targets` radius and study area boundaries apply to both Sponsored Pins and Contextual Banners.
