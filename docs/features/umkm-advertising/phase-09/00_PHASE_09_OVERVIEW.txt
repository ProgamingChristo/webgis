GETRA - UMKM INTELLIGENCE & ADVERTISING TRACK
PHASE 09: CONTEXTUAL PROMO BANNER & PROFILE POSTER
=====================================================

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Phase 09 expands GETRA's advertising placement ecosystem by introducing two dedicated, high-impact promotional surfaces:
- Contextual Promo Banner: Displays a single paid promotional card in the commuter Fair Discovery feed when the query context and spatial proximity match an active campaign's CONTEXTUAL_BANNER creative.
- Profile Poster: Displays a rich promotional banner strictly inside a merchant's own profile view when an active campaign's PROFILE_POSTER creative is ready.

2. CORE PILLARS & ARCHITECTURAL REUSE
Phase 09 adheres strictly to the canonical GETRA advertising foundation established across Phases 4 through 8:
- Zero Table Duplication: Reuses canonical `ad_campaigns`, `ad_creatives`, `ad_campaign_targets`, and `ad_campaign_schedules`. No duplicate banner or poster tables were introduced.
- Strict Placement Isolation: Contextual banners are capped to 1 per discovery context. Profile posters are strictly bound to the same merchant (`merchant_id` matching).
- Zero Analytics Side Effects: In accordance with Phase 09 specification, rendering or interacting with banners and posters produces zero database writes and zero analytics side effects. Impression and click tracking are deferred to Phase 10.
- Failure Isolation: If placement evaluation throws or times out, organic discovery and canonical merchant profile drawers load completely unaffected.
