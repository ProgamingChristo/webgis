GETRA - UMKM INTELLIGENCE & ADVERTISING TRACK
PHASE 09: ZERO EVENT TRACKING SIDE EFFECTS
=====================================================

1. STRICT ZERO SIDE EFFECT DIRECTIVE
Phase 09 explicitly prohibits database mutations during ad serving and rendering:
- NO impression logs are written to database tables
- NO click events are tracked or persisted
- NO budget or balance is deducted upon banner or poster display
- NO analytics event pipelines are invoked

2. AUDIT COMPLIANCE
- `ContextualBannerServingService.getEligibleBanner()` performs ONLY read queries (`SELECT`) on `ad_campaigns`, `merchants`, `ad_creatives`, and `ad_campaign_targets`.
- `ProfilePosterServingService.getProfilePosterForMerchant()` performs ONLY read queries (`SELECT`).
- `GET /api/advertising/placements/contextual-banner` is an idempotent read endpoint.
- `GET /api/merchants/[id]/advertising/profile-poster` is an idempotent read endpoint.
- Frontend components `<ContextualBanner />` and `<ProfilePoster />` trigger UI callbacks (such as routing and profile inspection) with zero event-logging HTTP requests.

3. FUTURE TRACKING
All impression logging, click tracking, CTR calculation, and real-time interaction metrics are strictly isolated to Phase 10.
