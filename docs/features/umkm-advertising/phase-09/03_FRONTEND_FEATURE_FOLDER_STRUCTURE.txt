GETRA - UMKM INTELLIGENCE & ADVERTISING TRACK
PHASE 09: FRONTEND FEATURE FOLDER STRUCTURE
=====================================================

1. FEATURE-FOLDER DIRECTORY LAYOUT
Frontend advertising features are structured modularly under `frontend/src/features/umkm-advertising/`:

frontend/src/features/umkm-advertising/
├── contextual-banner/
│   ├── components/
│   │   └── contextual-banner.tsx
│   ├── hooks/
│   │   └── use-contextual-banner.ts
│   ├── services/
│   │   └── contextual-banner.service.ts
│   ├── types/
│   │   └── contextual-banner.types.ts
│   └── index.ts
│
├── profile-poster/
│   ├── components/
│   │   └── profile-poster.tsx
│   ├── hooks/
│   │   └── use-profile-poster.ts
│   ├── services/
│   │   └── profile-poster.service.ts
│   ├── types/
│   │   └── profile-poster.types.ts
│   └── index.ts
│
├── index.ts (Public barrel export re-exporting all subdomains)

2. CONSUMING SURFACES
- `frontend/src/features/fair-discovery/components/fair-discovery-results.tsx`:
  Renders `<ContextualBanner />` below discovery disclosure when `result.contextual_banner` is present.
- `frontend/components/getra-dashboard.tsx`:
  Invokes `useProfilePoster({ merchantId })` and renders `<ProfilePoster />` in the right drawer "Detail lokasi".
