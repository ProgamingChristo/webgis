================================================================================
GETRA — GEO-ENABLED TRANSIT & RETAIL ANALYTICS
CAMPAIGN ANALYTICS — PRIVACY PROTECTION & DATA HYGIENE
================================================================================

1. ZERO PII & AGGREGATE ONLY EXPOSURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- The analytics API (/api/umkm/advertising/campaigns/{id}/analytics) NEVER
  exposes individual event rows, commuter user IDs, IP addresses, or device IDs.
- Only aggregated count numbers (timeseries buckets and placement breakdown)
  are returned to the merchant owner.

2. ZERO COMMUTER GPS MOVEMENT DISCLOSURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Route request metrics indicate only that a walking route calculation was
  requested towards the merchant's coordinate.
- The commuter's origin location, trajectory, speed, and ongoing transit history
  are strictly private and NEVER recorded in campaign_events or analytics aggregates.

3. CROSS-MERCHANT ISOLATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- A merchant owner cannot view analytics for campaigns belonging to any other
  merchant.
- Campaign ownership verification enforces `merchants.owner_id = user.id`.
