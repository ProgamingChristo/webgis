GETRA — GEO-ENABLED TRANSIT & RETAIL ANALYTICS
CAMPAIGN ANALYTICS — PHASE 11 ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW & SCOPE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Campaign Analytics Dashboard (/umkm/advertising/analytics) provides verified
UMKM merchant owners with clear, real-time reporting on how commuter transit audiences
interact with their advertising campaigns.

2. PIPELINE & TOPOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 10 Append-Only Events:
[Map / Banner / Drawer UI]
       │
       ▼ (Fire-and-forget beacon with session-safe dedup)
[POST /api/advertising/events]
       │
       ▼ (Append-only insert)
[public.campaign_events] (IMPRESSION, SPONSORED_PIN_CLICK, PROFILE_OPEN, ROUTE_REQUEST)

Phase 11 Analytics Consumption:
[Client: AnalyticsDashboard (/umkm/advertising/analytics)]
       │
       ▼ GET /api/umkm/advertising/campaigns/{id}/analytics?from=...&to=...&placement=...
[Backend: CampaignAnalyticsService]
       │ (1. Verified Merchant Ownership Check)
       ▼ (2. Aggregation Repository)
[SQL Aggregation Engine]
       │ ├── Summary Totals & Derived CTR
       │ ├── Timeseries Grouping by YYYY-MM-DD
       │ └── Placement Breakdown (SPONSORED_PIN, CONTEXTUAL_BANNER, PROFILE_POSTER)
       ▼
[JSON Analytics Response Payload] -> [Visual Dashboard Render]
