================================================================================
GETRA — GEO-ENABLED TRANSIT & RETAIL ANALYTICS
CAMPAIGN ANALYTICS — METRICS DICTIONARY
================================================================================

1. CANONICAL CAMPAIGN INTERACTION METRICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Metric Name          | Event Type Source    | Calculation / Definition
---------------------|----------------------|---------------------------------------------------------
Impressions          | IMPRESSION           | Count of times ad placement entered client viewport/screen.
Sponsored Pin Clicks | SPONSORED_PIN_CLICK  | Count of direct click interactions on map sponsored pins.
Profile Opens        | PROFILE_OPEN         | Count of campaign-attributed merchant profile drawer opens.
Route Requests       | ROUTE_REQUEST        | Count of walking route navigation requests initiated from ad.
Sponsored Pin CTR    | Derived              | (SPONSORED_PIN_CLICK / SPONSORED_PIN IMPRESSIONS) * 100%

2. DISCLAIMERS & SCOPE BOUNDARIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- GETRA Analytics strictly measures commuter digital interaction events.
- It DOES NOT measure cash register transactions, offline footfall counts,
  or financial Return on Investment (ROI).
- Disclaimer displayed on all UI views:
  "Analytics GETRA mengukur interaksi campaign nyata, bukan penjualan atau ROI."
