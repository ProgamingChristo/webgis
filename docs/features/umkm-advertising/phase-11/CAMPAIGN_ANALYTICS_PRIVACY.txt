1. COMMUTER PRIVACY GUARANTEES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. No Personal Identifiable Information (Zero PII):
   - The analytics API and frontend report aggregate counts only.
   - User identities (`auth.users.id`), emails, names, and IP addresses are NEVER
     stored in `public.campaign_events` nor returned by the analytics service.

2. No Commuter Movement / GPS Tracking:
   - Route request metrics indicate only that a routing computation was triggered
     towards the merchant.
   - The origin point, route path, commute frequency, and travel speed of commuters
     are strictly private and NEVER recorded or disclosed.

3. Merchant Isolation:
   - Campaign reporting is strictly sandboxed to the authenticated merchant owner.
