# GETRA Context

GETRA is a spatial decision support product for transit-area accessibility and local economic opportunity in Jakarta. This glossary keeps product language precise across code, documents, and presentations.

## Language

**GETRA**:
Geo-Enabled Transit & Retail Analytics, a WebGIS-based spatial decision support system for commuter access, UMKM visibility, investment location comparison, and public planning around transit areas.
_Avoid_: Generic UMKM app, map app

**Spatial Decision Support System**:
A product that turns location, network, demand, supply, and access data into explainable decisions for different stakeholders.
_Avoid_: Dashboard, chatbot, plain map

**Commuter**:
A transit user who needs reachable services from a station or stop based on walking time, budget, category, opening status, and accessibility needs.
_Avoid_: Customer, passenger only

**UMKM**:
A small local business whose discoverability depends on profile quality, location accuracy, walking access, local demand, and competition around transit.
_Avoid_: Merchant only, seller only

**Investor**:
A stakeholder comparing candidate business locations based on access, demand, supply, competition, and retail gap indicators.
_Avoid_: Advertiser, property buyer

**Pemerintah Daerah**:
A planning stakeholder using aggregate spatial indicators to prioritize service equity, pedestrian access improvements, and area intervention.
_Avoid_: Admin

**Service Area**:
The reachable area from a transit origin within a walking-time threshold calculated by a pedestrian network.
_Avoid_: Radius, buffer, catchment when network time is intended

**Accessibility Score**:
An explainable score for how reachable a business or area is, based on walking time, network distance, route comfort, and access barriers.
_Avoid_: Rating, popularity score

**Retail Gap**:
The mismatch between local demand and available supply for a category within a transit-area context.
_Avoid_: Sales forecast, guaranteed opportunity

**Opportunity Zone**:
An area where demand, accessibility, and supply conditions indicate a potential service or business gap.
_Avoid_: Investment recommendation, profit guarantee

**Fair Discovery**:
Ranking behavior that keeps organic recommendations grounded in relevance and access while preventing popularity or paid placement from dominating every result.
_Avoid_: Sponsored ranking, random rotation

**Hidden Gem**:
A relevant and reachable local place that deserves exposure despite lower popularity or weaker platform visibility.
_Avoid_: Sponsored place, viral place

**Grounded AI**:
AI that parses user intent or explains structured geospatial results without inventing coordinates, routes, scores, demand, or factual claims.
_Avoid_: AI as GIS engine, AI-generated spatial data

**Community Mapping**:
Location-bound contributions from users about businesses, access barriers, local findings, requests, or cultural context.
_Avoid_: Social feed, comments
